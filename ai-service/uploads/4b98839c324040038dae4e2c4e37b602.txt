### Vijay Mallya – Summary

Vijay Mallya is an Indian businessman who was once known as the “King of Good Times” because of his luxurious lifestyle and his ownership of several prominent businesses. He was the former chairman of **United Breweries Group** and became widely known through the **Kingfisher** brand of beer. He also entered the aviation industry by launching **Kingfisher Airlines** in 2005.

Kingfisher Airlines initially became popular for its service and branding, but the company later faced serious financial difficulties. It accumulated large debts and eventually stopped operations in 2012. Banks and financial institutions subsequently alleged that loans taken by companies associated with Mallya had not been repaid.
